🌦️ Sowing & Flood Window Guide

1. Early Recession (Sept End – Oct Mid)

Condition: Baadh ka paani jaldi utar gaya, gaad nami barabar hai.
Best Crops: Sarson (Mustard), Agaiti Makka, Matar, aur Sabziyan (Karela, Parwal, Baingan).

2. Mid Recession (Oct Mid – Nov End)

Condition: Koshi aur Ganga diara mein buwai ka sabse golden window.
Best Crops: Gehu (Shriram Super / HD-2967), Rabi Makka, Chana, Masoor.

3. Late Recession (Dec – Jan)

Condition: Nichli zameen jahan paani der se utra.
Best Crops: Late-sown Gehu (DBW-14 / HD-3059) Zero-Tillage vidhi se, ya Garma Makka & Moong (Feb mein).

4. Non-Flooded Upland / Orchard Land

Condition: Bina baadh wali oonchi zameen ya Aam ke bagiche ke beech.
Best Crops: Kharif mein Dhan/Arhar/Makka, Rabi mein Gehu/Sarson, aur Bagiche mein Haldi/Adrak/Sabzi intercropping.
